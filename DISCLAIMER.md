# Disclaimer

**Holdnote is not financial advice.** It is a free research tool for
personal use. It reads public documents (regulatory filings, fund
factsheets, key information documents and issuers' holdings files) and lays
out what they say in tables. It never recommends buying, selling or holding
anything, never says what a price will do, and never suggests how much to
invest.

**It can be wrong.** Documents are misread, issuers change their files,
data goes stale, and software has bugs. Every report names its sources so
you can check them. Before making any decision that matters, read the
original documents yourself, and consider talking to a licensed financial
adviser in your country.
